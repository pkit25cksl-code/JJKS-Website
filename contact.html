Frequently asked questions

How much does a project cost?

Every project gets a transparent, fixed-scope quote after a free consultation, so you know the cost before anything starts. Introductory rates or pilot work may be available in exchange for a testimonial.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size, but we work in short sprints and you'll see working software early. We'll give you a realistic timeline in your quote, and we'll tell you honestly if something won't fit.

How involved do I need to be?

A short review at the end of each sprint is usually enough. Your feedback shapes the next sprint, so your time makes a real difference to the result.

Can I maintain the software myself afterwards?

Yes. Every project ends with a handover and plain-English documentation, so you aren't dependent on us.

What happens to my details?

We collect only what we need to reply and never share or sell it. See our Privacy policy for how long we keep it and your rights.
