Services

Software that fits the way you work

We build focused tools for organisations too small for a big agency, in clear, affordable increments.

Web applications

Tools that run in any browser, on any device, with nothing to install. Ideal when your customers or staff need access from anywhere.

Good for

  • Online booking and appointment systems
  • Simple ordering and pre-order tools
  • Stock and inventory tracking for small shops and trades
  • Promotional and information websites

What you get

  • A mobile-first, accessible site built to WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Working software at the end of every sprint
  • Plain-English documentation and a handover session

Desktop and mobile apps

Dependable applications for the day-to-day running of community groups, charities and small teams.

Good for

  • Membership and subscription records
  • Volunteer rotas and availability
  • Event sign-ups and attendance
  • Internal tools that replace messy spreadsheets

What you get

  • Software designed around your existing process
  • Simple maintenance, so you aren't dependent on us
  • Your data handled in line with UK GDPR

Our process

The same five steps for every project, large or small.

  1. Discover

    We listen, ask questions and play your brief back to you so nothing is lost.

  2. Prototype

    A clickable prototype lets you try the idea before any code is written.

  3. Build

    Short sprints, each ending in working software you can see and test.

  4. Review

    Your feedback shapes the next sprint. Changes are agreed in writing.

  5. Hand over

    Plain-English documentation so you can run and maintain what we built.
