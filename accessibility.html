Accessibility statement

Last updated: 14 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, and accessibility checks are part of our definition of done.

What we've done

  • Text and important interface elements meet AA colour contrast. Body text is at least 16px.
  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Pages use a logical heading structure and landmarks for screen readers.
  • Every form field has a visible label, and errors are explained in text, not colour alone.
  • Layouts adapt from small phones to large screens and work when zoomed to 400%.
  • Decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers; meaningful illustrations have text alternatives.
  • Animation is kept minimal and switched off if you've asked your device to reduce motion.

How we test

Before each release we run automated checks (including Lighthouse), test keyboard navigation, and ask someone who didn't build the page to review it. Results are recorded in our team minutes.

Known limitations

Illustrations of our project work are simplified drawings rather than real screenshots, while we wait for client permission to publish the originals.

Feedback

If you find anything difficult to use, please email info@jjks.com. Tell us the page and the problem, and we'll aim to reply within 2 working days.

This statement was prepared on 14 October 2026.
