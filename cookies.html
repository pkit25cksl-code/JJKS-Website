Cookie notice

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This website does not set any cookies. We don't use advertising, tracking pixels or third-party analytics cookies, which is why you haven't seen a cookie banner.

Fonts and scripts

Our font (Open Sans) is stored on our own site rather than loaded from Google, so your visit isn't shared with a font provider. Our only script powers the mobile menu, the portfolio filter and form checks, and stores nothing on your device.

Forms

When you send an enquiry or join the newsletter, your details go to our form and newsletter providers, as described in our Privacy policy. Submitting a form from this site does not place cookies on your device.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics, we'll choose a cookie-free, privacy-friendly tool and update this notice first. If we add anything that needs consent, we'll ask before it runs.
