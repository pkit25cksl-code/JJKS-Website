About us

From an idea to working software

JJKS is a team of Computer Science students at the University of Sunderland. We build custom software for small businesses and community groups in short agile cycles, keep our clients involved, and document what we build so they can maintain it.

Our mission

To turn small organisations' ideas into reliable, easy-to-use software through open communication, honest planning and regular delivery.

Our vision

A region where every small business and community group can afford software that fits the way they work.

We believe in helping clients find the light at the end of the tunnel, from an idea to working software. That's where our tagline comes from: software that lights the way.

Our values

Clarity

We explain technical decisions in plain English and agree scope in writing.

Collaboration

You see and test work at every sprint, and your feedback shapes the next one.

Craft

We test, document and review our work to professional standards.

Integrity

We're honest about what we can deliver, and we protect your data and confidentiality.

How we work

Discover, prototype, build, review and hand over, with you involved at every step.

  1. Discover

    We listen, ask questions and play your brief back to you so nothing is lost.

  2. Prototype

    A clickable prototype lets you try the idea before any code is written.

  3. Build

    Short sprints, each ending in working software you can see and test.

  4. Review

    Your feedback shapes the next sprint. Changes are agreed in writing.

  5. Hand over

    Plain-English documentation so you can run and maintain what we built.
