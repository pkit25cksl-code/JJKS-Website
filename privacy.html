Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data JJKS collects through this website, why, and what your rights are. We follow the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and guidance from the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Who we are

JJKS is a student-led software team at the University of Sunderland. For any privacy question, email info@jjks.com.

What we collect and why

WhenWhat we collectWhyLawful basis
You send an enquiryName, email, organisation and your messageTo reply and discuss your projectConsent (the tick-box on the form)
You join the newsletterEmail addressTo send one short email a monthConsent
You visit the siteStandard technical logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP address and browser type)To keep the site secure and workingLegitimate interests

We collect the minimum we need. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes your data for us

  • Formspree receives contact-form submissions and forwards them to our inbox.
  • Buttondown stores newsletter subscriber addresses and sends the newsletter.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.

Some of these providers may store data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK GDPR.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: up to 12 months after our last contact, unless you become a client.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe, using the link in every email.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent at any time, or object to how we use it. Email us and we'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies. See our Cookie notice.
